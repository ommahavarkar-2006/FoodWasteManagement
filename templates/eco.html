♻️ Eco Tips for Food Waste Management

1. Buy Only What You Need

Plan meals and make a shopping list to avoid buying extra food that later gets thrown away.

2. Store Food Properly

Use airtight containers, refrigerate leftovers, and learn proper storage techniques to keep food fresh longer.

3. Check Expiry Dates Regularly

Organize your fridge (FIFO – First In, First Out) so older items get used before newer ones.

4. Use Leftovers Creatively

Transform leftover rice, vegetables, or bread into new dishes like fried rice, soups, and sandwiches.

5. Compost Organic Waste

Convert fruit peels, vegetable scraps, and coffee grounds into nutrient-rich compost for plants.

6. Freeze Extra Food

Freeze cut vegetables, fruits, bread, and meals to extend their life and avoid spoiling.

7. Donate Surplus Food

Share edible extra food with neighbors, community fridges, or local NGOs.

8. Measure Portions Carefully

Cook only the amount you actually need to reduce leftover food.

9. Use Peels & Scraps

Many peels (potato, carrot, apple) can be cooked or used in soups and stocks.

10. Track Your Waste

Keep a small notebook or app to track what food gets wasted — avoid repeating mistakes.
