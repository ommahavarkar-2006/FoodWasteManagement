Transform Food Waste Into Impact

Join users reducing waste, saving money, and fighting hunger

70M

Tonnes Wasted Annually in India. India wastes around 70 million tonnes of food every year. This happens during farming, transportation, storage, restaurants, and homes. Food waste also increases methane emissions, which is bad for the environment.

200M

People Suffer from Malnutrition. Around 200 million Indians, including women and children, suffer from malnutrition. While huge quantities of food are wasted, millions struggle without daily meals. Reducing food waste can help bridge this gap.

₹1 Lakh Cr

Economic Loss Per Year. Food wastage costs India nearly ₹1,00,000 crore every year. This includes loss of food, labour, water, electricity, transport, and storage.

45%

Total Food Generation Wasted. Almost 45% of the food produced in India gets wasted before consumption. Farm losses, storage issues, packaging problems, and household leftovers contribute to this.

About Our Mission

In India, around 70 million tonnes of food is wasted annually, amounting to 45% of total food generated. This translates into an economic loss of around ₹1 lakh crore rupees. Meanwhile, 200 million people suffer from malnutrition, including 50 million children. We believe this paradox must end.


Our platform connects surplus food from restaurants, hotels, and households with communities in need. Through smart technology, awareness programs, and local partnerships, we're building a zero-waste future where no meal goes to waste and no one goes hungry.
